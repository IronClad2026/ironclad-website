# Caster programme privacy decisions for launch

The existing legal corpus explains account closure, protected competition history, and when factual records may remain under the Former Competitor label. The programme adds private application review, profile media and independently verified casting attribution. Approve a programme notice and a matching Privacy Policy update before public activation. Record the effective document version through the existing legal publication process.

The notice should explain what applicants provide, why Discord and country are requested, who can see reviewer notes and uploaded media, which profile fields can become public, and what happens to casting attribution when a profile is hidden, a membership ends, or an account closes. Explain that clicking a provider player sends requests to YouTube or Twitch and that CDN or third-party copies already delivered cannot be recalled.

| Record | Proposed operational decision for approval |
| --- | --- |
| Expired or revoked invitations | Keep nonsecret issuance and disposition metadata for 90 days for abuse review. Expire access immediately. Raw invitation values are never stored. |
| Abandoned applications | Close after 90 days of inactivity, with a reminder where approved. Remove private content and unreferenced assets after a further 30-day recovery window. |
| Rejected or withdrawn applications | Keep private evidence for a 90-day review and appeal window. Retain only a minimum decision record after that window, subject to any active dispute. |
| Private and replaced media | Stop public delivery at replacement, unpublication or closure. Remove unreferenced objects after 30 days, once references and any documented holds are checked. Published or current private drafts can still reference an older object. |
| Superseded revisions | Keep a bounded recovery and moderation history, with 90 days as the proposed starting point. Preserve a minimal decision receipt when it is still needed after the draft and image copies are removed. |
| Administrator audit records | Start with a 12-month review window. Retain only necessary action, timing and authority evidence. Document any hold, its purpose, access and review date. |
| Inactive or suspended casters | Remove current promotional visibility immediately. Agree a 90-day reactivation window for the private working profile and a separate decision for prolonged suspension or an open dispute. |
| Closed accounts | Remove current access, promotion and profile media delivery immediately. Define which private fields and objects are erased, which minimal closure evidence remains, and how removal propagates to backups. |
| Historical casting attribution | Retain the minimum factual event, scope and verification dates where needed. Default to the neutral Former Caster label on closed accounts, without avatar, profile links, identity handles or private contact information. Any continuing named public credit needs a distinct approved rule and a removal request path. |

These proposed periods are working decisions, subject to the platform’s review of applicable obligations, contractual needs, appeals, and lawful holds. A hold should be time-bounded and apply only to affected records. Review the [OAIC security, destruction and de-identification guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information).

Before approving a cleanup job, inventory every storage and database reference, test a dry run against synthetic accounts, and make a held or actively referenced record ineligible. Publish an owner-approved notice and approve effective document versions before launch. Staging validation preserves records for review.
