## Proposed programme notice

Place the first paragraph before the applicant submits their application. Place the remaining paragraphs beside the corresponding Studio controls and link to the full notice from the application form and invitation.

> IronClad uses the information you provide to assess your Caster Programme application, contact you through the platform, support tournament video publication and protect the integrity of tournament coverage. Authorised staff can review your submitted information, images and links. Your account email and review conversations are private. Discord is optional and is used for review or contact when provided. We use your country during review and show it on an approved public caster profile only if you choose to display it. A caster invitation or application does not require a competitive player profile or affect your ability to compete.
>
> Publishing a profile makes the profile information, media and links shown in the public preview available on IronClad. This can include your display name, languages, games, biography, public links and verified casting credits. You can hide your country in Studio. If your account closes, IronClad removes current profile, associated media delivery and promotion. Verified event records may remain with the neutral label “Former Caster” after the attribution controls described in our Privacy Policy are in effect. You can request a correction or removal of identifying information through the privacy contact in that policy.
>
> When you publish a tournament video, IronClad shows the video title, optional description, recording link and associated tournament, division or match on your caster profile and in Tournament Media. You can unpublish your own recording from both places. Authorised staff can hide or correct a recording and retain the necessary action record. Self-published videos carry no administrator-verified coverage designation.
>
> A video player loads only when you choose to load it. YouTube or Twitch then receives the information needed to deliver its player, which may include your network address, device and browser information. That provider applies its own privacy terms. You can use the separate watch link to open the provider directly. Where a browser or provider blocks playback, the watch link remains available.

The Former Caster sentence is proposed wording for launch after the technical dependency below is closed. Until then the exact operational statement is “IronClad removes closed profiles from public display and preserves internal verified event records as needed for tournament integrity.”

## Proposed retention and erasure schedule

| Category | Retained information, purpose and access | Clock and disposition | Implementation status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invitations and continuations | Digest, lifecycle timestamps, optional non-identifying reference and minimum issuance/claim audit. Only the service and authorised programme staff can inspect applicable records. | Invite expires after seven days. Continuation expires within 30 minutes and may be consumed earlier. Remove expired/revoked non-claimed invitation material 90 days after expiry or revocation. Minimise claimed references after linked review ends while retaining a minimal audit event. | Expiry, revocation, secure cookie, bounded opportunistic expired-continuation cleanup and consumption exist. Durable invitation minimisation and periodic cleanup require implementation. |
| Applications and reviewer information | Applicant draft, submitted fields, private contacts and reviewer comments. Owner and authorised reviewers have scoped access. Account identifiers and raw review notes have different retention purposes. | Treat a draft as abandoned after 90 days without activity. Allow 30 days for correction or restoration, then erase content and contact details. For withdrawn or rejected applications, allow 90 days from final decision for review/appeal, then erase content and minimise the decision receipt. Keep the latest necessary approved evidence while membership is active. | Private access and versioned decisions exist. Abandonment, withdrawal windows and erasure require implementation. An appeal pauses the affected timer. |
| Uploaded media | Private uploads and the versions actually referenced by applications or publications. The owner and scoped staff can view private items. Only current approved assets reach visitors. | Stop public delivery on hide, replacement or closure. Erase unreferenced blobs and storage metadata 30 days after the last legitimate reference is removed. Avoid deleting shared objects still referenced by a current draft or review. | Scoped delivery and public removal exist. Reference-aware deletion and backup reconciliation require implementation. |
| Submitted and published revisions | Frozen application snapshots and publication evidence needed for appeal, moderation and dispute resolution. Staff and the owner see only their authorised scope. | Keep superseded content for 90 days from replacement or final decision. Remove content and unreferenced media after the window. Retain a minimal decision receipt where required. Keep the current published revision through active publication. | Immutable revision separation exists. Revision minimisation and removal require implementation. |
| Moderation and audit | Action category, time, accountable operator, reason and subject link where necessary. Restricted programme and privacy staff only. | Keep identifiable entries for 12 months from the last relevant action, then erase unnecessary content and remove direct identifiers. Keep a minimal non-identifying integrity receipt where required. Private feedback follows the application window. | Authorised audit access and append-only decisions exist. Minimisation, role review and retention job require implementation. |
| Tournament video publication | One canonical video link and its author, scope and visibility record. Public views show the video title, optional description, recording link and eligible competition context while the publication and caster remain visible. Staff can inspect associated moderation records. | Immediately withdraw both public views on unpublication, profile hide, suspension or account closure. Keep private unpublication or moderation evidence for 90 days from the final action, then remove identifying publication metadata when no continuing purpose applies. Apply the separate audit window and case holds. | Shared visibility controls and audit records exist. Time-based minimisation, deletion, backup reconciliation and hold-aware disposition require implementation and approval. |
| Inactive or suspended memberships | Minimum membership state, private profile and evidence needed for reactivation or review. Owner access remains governed by the membership state. No public promotion. | Allow 90 days from inactivity or final suspension decision for reactivation or appeal. Then apply the revision and media windows and retain the minimum membership record if needed for historical integrity. | Visibility and reinstatement controls exist. Inactivity expiry and data disposition require implementation. |
| Closed accounts | The minimum closure marker, legally necessary receipts and verified event history. Access is restricted to staff with an operational, security or legal purpose. | Invalidate access and public promotion at closure. After 30 days, erase unneeded Caster contacts, drafts, private/replaced blobs and linking fields, subject to active legal requirements. Apply the audit window to necessary review evidence. Reapply verified erasure after a restoration. | Session, write, promotion and media gates passed on Staging. Durable Caster erasure, ongoing backup reconciliation and closed-case operations require implementation. |
| Historical coverage and former casters | Keep event, division or match, date, verified outcome and integrity receipt. Public archive fields are distinct from restricted verification. | Keep the minimum factual verification needed for event history. Ordinary caster publications withdraw from both public views on account closure and do not become verified historical credits. On closure, use neutral attribution where a public archive entry remains. Remove profile links and direct identifiers from public fields. Review links and credits that identify the former caster when a correction is requested. | Current application suppresses closed-caster promotion and keeps internal event audit. Public neutral attribution requires a separate implementation and approval. |

Allow only a recorded, case-specific hold for a dispute, appeal, safety investigation or applicable legal requirement. Record its owner, minimum affected records and next review date. Restrict access, suspend only the affected disposition and resume the timer when the hold ends. This supersedes the Phase 5 draft where it left account closure, linked invitations, reviewer evidence and inactive membership ambiguous. It preserves the proposed 90-day review and 30-day recovery windows while anchoring each to a specific event.
