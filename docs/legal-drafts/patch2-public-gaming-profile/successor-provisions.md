# IronClad public gaming profiles and tournament information

**Review draft. Effective date pending.** These proposed changes use Rulebook 3.2, Player Participation Agreement 3.2, Terms 1.1 and Privacy Policy 1.3 as predecessors. Carry forward unrelated content verbatim. The existing documents and recorded acceptances still apply. The temporary public-profile arrangement ends with HOLD THE LINE.

## Rulebook 3.3

**19.2** Gaming profiles created after the new policy takes effect under the current Terms are public. Visitors can see published gaming profiles without signing in. The Privacy Policy describes how existing profiles are treated. Factual competition participation remains part of the official Competition History and follows the documents applicable to each tournament.

**19.3** Public Discord contact requires a separate choice. Showcase and optional media remain subject to their existing publication and moderation controls. Private match chat, assistance, administrative material and restricted match evidence remain protected.

## Player Participation Agreement 3.3

**14.3** Public Competition History may include the Player’s approved competition name, Tournament participation, Division, brackets, official matches and results, placements and standings. Separate gaming-profile information follows the current Terms and Privacy Policy, including their existing-profile transition. An authorized read-only competition integration may receive a verified Relic profile identifier and the IronClad profile link where the profile may be published. Discord and optional media have their own controls.

**20.2** Privacy acknowledgement records that the Player was shown the identified Privacy Policy. The gaming-profile notice records what the account holder was shown. A separate publication choice for an existing profile, public Discord and optional media permissions each follow their own applicable terms. Previously accepted documents and historical acceptance records remain available for verification.

## Terms 1.2

**Section 8, replacement for the final profile paragraph.** If you create a gaming profile after this policy takes effect under the current Terms and Privacy Policy, it is public. Visitors can view it without signing in. Existing profiles keep their documented visibility under the transition described in the Privacy Policy. A player with an existing profile may separately choose to publish it or make it private. An unresolved privacy objection restricts profile publication while IronClad reviews it. Publication of the gaming profile is separate from official Competition History. Discord visibility and optional media permissions follow their own controls. Account closure may remove or pseudonymise personal identifiers while preserving required competition records, as described in the Privacy Policy.

**Section 12, operational licence list.** Retain the commitment to honour applicable visibility choices. It covers earlier-account publication choices, separate contact settings and optional content controls.

## Privacy Policy 1.4

**Section 3, profile and contact information.** Replace the visibility-settings item with “historical profile visibility settings, applicable publication choices, and the minimum evidence needed to administer the existing-profile transition”. Add the verified canonical Relic profile identifier under Steam and Relic information. Add a private evidence entry for “gaming-profile notice version, acknowledgement time and a protected reference to the authenticated account”. Describe any account-controlled publication record separately from the notice receipt. Carry forward current private assistance, match communications, account acceptance, retention and security evidence provisions.

**Section 5, purposes.** Replace “create and maintain profiles” with “create and maintain public gaming profiles and private account management under the applicable visibility rules”. Replace “publish factual Competition History” with “publish factual Competition History and, where applicable, connect verified public profile identity through supported competition integrations”.

**Section 6, publication and choices.** Gaming-profile publication follows section 8. An existing player’s publication choice, public Discord and optional promotional media are separate choices. Acknowledgement records the information shown. Withdrawal of an optional choice leaves historical tournament facts subject to their applicable rules. Each disclosure remains subject to its applicable privacy basis and rights.

**Section 8, profiles and transition.** Gaming profiles created after the policy takes effect are public when the account has accepted the current Terms and acknowledged the current Privacy Policy. This also applies when an older account creates its first profile under the new policy. Existing profiles keep any prior publication only within its verified scope. If the account owner previously turned publication off or the earlier choice cannot be established, the full profile stays restricted. A player can separately choose to publish an existing profile or make it private again. If the owner never returns, the earlier treatment remains in place. While a privacy objection is unresolved, IronClad restricts full-profile publication, including for newer profiles. An acknowledgement alone leaves the profile’s visibility unchanged. Factual competition participation and results remain subject to the governing documents for each event.

Avatar files stay in private Storage. When a profile is eligible for public display, the player-ID route can show its avatar without exposing the private object path. Discord is optional and appears only when the player turns on its separate visibility setting. Clearing Discord disables its public visibility. An optional Discord username is unnecessary for profile completion and registration.

**Section 9, verification and integration.** Where trusted verification establishes a canonical Relic profile identifier, IronClad records the binding. The authorized CoH3Stats integration can receive verified identity information and a public IronClad profile link within the scope described in section 15. IronClad does not assign guessed identifiers. Preserve the distinctions between Steam ownership, Relic eligibility verification and outward integration access.

**Section 14, public information.** Visitors can see published information without signing in. An eligible profile can show the public IronClad player identifier, display name, in-game name, country, competitive region, rating, profile creation time, avatar availability and avatar. Published Showcase content, badges and eligible moderated clips can appear under their existing controls. Official event information may show approved competition names, participation, divisions, brackets, official matches and scores, results, placements, standings and recorded tournament ratings where supported. Published Decisions and expressly public anonymous Poll totals retain their existing rules. Public Discord follows the separate choice described above. Profile restrictions and closure preserve factual history and follow existing pseudonymisation and retention protections. Public pages exclude email, credentials, private account information, private match chat and assistance, restricted replay evidence and private Storage paths. Public information can be copied or indexed by others outside IronClad’s control.

**Section 15, providers and recipients.** Add “CoH3Stats: authorized read-only access to supported competition data, including a verified canonical Relic profile identifier and the IronClad profile link for eligible public profiles. This integration has no authority to verify eligibility or change competition records.” Preserve existing provider, retention and transfer disclosures unless implementation evidence requires a separate revision.

**Section 20, privacy rights.** Keep the existing rights and contact route. Describe the existing-profile publication choice and the separate Discord control. An unresolved objection restricts full-profile publication pending review, including for accounts subject to the new policy. The outcome is recorded separately from any acknowledgement. Existing closure, restriction, backup, redaction and legal-hold provisions continue to apply.
