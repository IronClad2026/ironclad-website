# IronClad public gaming profiles and tournament information

**Review draft. Effective date pending.** These proposed changes use Rulebook 3.2, Player Participation Agreement 3.2, Terms 1.1 and Privacy Policy 1.3 as predecessors. Carry forward unrelated content verbatim. The existing documents and recorded acceptances still apply. The temporary public-profile arrangement ends with HOLD THE LINE.

## Rulebook 3.3

**19.2** Visitors can see core gaming profiles without signing in, subject to the Privacy Policy and individual privacy restrictions. Official Competition History includes the supported public tournament facts described in the Privacy Policy and follows the documents applicable to each tournament.

**19.3** Players can choose to share optional profile details. Public Discord contact requires its own separate choice. Showcase and clips remain subject to their existing publication and moderation controls. Private match chat, assistance, administrative material and restricted match evidence remain protected.

## Player Participation Agreement 3.3

**14.3** Public Competition History may include the Player’s approved competition name, Tournament participation, Division, recorded roster ELO, brackets, official matches and results, placements and standings. Core gaming-profile information follows the Terms and Privacy Policy. An authorized read-only competition integration may receive a verified Relic profile identifier and a public IronClad profile link only where the applicable optional choice or verified prior permission covers that disclosure. Discord and optional media have their own controls.

**20.2** Privacy acknowledgement records that the Player was shown the identified Privacy Policy. The gaming-profile notice records what the account holder was shown. Optional profile choices, public Discord and optional media permissions each follow their own terms. Previously accepted documents and historical acceptance records remain available for verification.

## Terms 1.2

**Section 8, replacement for the final profile paragraph.** After the new policy takes effect, visitors can view core information on existing and new gaming profiles without signing in. The Privacy Policy explains the exact fields and the separate choice to share optional details. Earlier profile settings remain in the records. Optional details stay private unless the player separately chooses to share them or verified prior permission covers them. An unresolved, recorded privacy objection restricts the affected gaming profile while IronClad reviews it. Discord and optional media follow their own controls. Official Competition History follows the applicable tournament documents. Account closure may remove or pseudonymise identifiers while preserving required competition records.

**Section 12, operational licence list.** Retain the commitment to honour applicable visibility choices. It covers optional profile details, separate contact settings and optional content controls.

## Privacy Policy 1.4

**Section 3, profile and contact information.** Replace the visibility-settings item with “historical profile visibility settings, optional profile disclosure choices and the minimum evidence needed to apply those choices or review an individual restriction”. Add the verified canonical Relic profile identifier under Steam and Relic information. Add a private evidence entry for “gaming-profile notice version, acknowledgement time and a protected reference to the authenticated account”. Describe optional disclosure records separately from the notice receipt. Carry forward current private assistance, match communications, account acceptance, retention and security evidence provisions.

**Section 5, purposes.** Replace “create and maintain profiles” with “create and maintain public gaming profiles and private account management under the applicable visibility rules”. Replace “publish factual Competition History” with “publish factual Competition History and, where applicable, connect verified public profile identity through supported competition integrations”.

**Section 6, publication and choices.** Core gaming-profile publication follows section 8. Sharing optional profile details, showing public Discord and allowing optional promotional media are separate choices. Acknowledgement records the information shown. Withdrawal of an optional choice leaves official tournament facts subject to their applicable rules. Each disclosure remains subject to its applicable privacy basis and rights.

**Section 8, profiles and transition.** From the effective date, visitors can see core information on active gaming profiles, including profiles created before that date. Earlier settings remain in the records. An off or unknown earlier setting does not prevent display of core information under this policy. Optional details stay private for existing and new profiles unless a separate player choice or verified prior permission covers them. Players can withdraw their optional choice. A player’s absence or acknowledgement alone does not grant optional permission. IronClad restricts the gaming profile for an individual account while a recorded privacy objection remains unresolved. Factual competition participation and results remain subject to the governing documents for each event.

Avatar files stay in private Storage. The player-ID route can show an avatar on a public gaming profile when the optional disclosure choice or verified prior permission covers it. The route does not expose the private object path. Discord appears only on a publicly visible gaming profile and only when the player turns on its separate visibility setting. Clearing Discord disables its public visibility. A Discord username is unnecessary for profile completion and registration.

**Section 9, verification and integration.** Where trusted verification establishes a canonical Relic profile identifier, IronClad records the binding. The authorized CoH3Stats integration can receive that identifier and its public IronClad profile link only when the optional disclosure choice or verified prior permission covers the link and the gaming profile is public. IronClad does not assign guessed identifiers. The integration does not verify eligibility.

**Section 14, public information.** Visitors can see published information without signing in. A core gaming profile can show the public IronClad player identifier and link, in-game name, competitive region or Division, and current ELO. Optional details include a different display name, country, avatar and avatar availability, published Showcase content and eligible moderated clips, and other non-official event snapshots. These need an optional disclosure choice or verified prior permission covering them. Official event information may show approved competition names, participation, Divisions, recorded roster ELO, brackets, official matches and scores, results, placements, standings and tournament history. Published Decisions and expressly public anonymous Poll totals retain their existing rules. Public Discord follows its separate setting. Profile restrictions and closure follow existing pseudonymisation and retention protections. Public pages exclude email addresses, Clerk user identifiers, authentication information, account creation time, sensitive personal information, private match chat and assistance, internal admin notes, restricted replay evidence and private Storage paths. Public information can be copied or indexed by others outside IronClad’s control.

**Section 15, providers and recipients.** Add “CoH3Stats: authorized read-only access to supported competition data. The integration may receive the verified canonical Relic profile identifier and its public IronClad profile link when the profile is public and the optional disclosure choice or verified prior permission covers that use. The Relic identifier does not appear on public pages. This integration has no authority to verify eligibility or change competition records.” Preserve existing provider, retention and transfer disclosures unless implementation evidence requires a separate revision.

**Section 20, privacy rights.** Keep the existing rights and contact route. Describe withdrawal of optional disclosure and the separate Discord control. An unresolved, recorded privacy objection restricts the individual gaming profile pending review. The outcome is recorded separately from any acknowledgement. Existing closure, restriction, backup, redaction and legal-hold provisions continue to apply.
