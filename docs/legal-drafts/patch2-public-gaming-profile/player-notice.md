# Draft player notice for approval

**Subject** Public gaming profiles after HOLD THE LINE

We plan to update IronClad’s Terms and Privacy Policy after HOLD THE LINE. Visitors can see public gaming profiles and official tournament information without signing in.

If you create a gaming profile under the new Terms after the policy takes effect, it will be public. It can show your display name, in-game name, country, competitive region, rating, avatar and any Showcase content you publish. The Privacy Policy lists the public information and explains the limited use of verified gaming identifiers by our competition integration. You can choose separately whether to show your Discord contact.

If you already have a profile, we will preserve its documented visibility. If you turned publication off, we cannot establish your earlier choice, or you have an unresolved privacy objection, your full profile stays restricted. If you do nothing, your existing profile keeps its treatment. You can separately choose to publish your existing profile or make it private again. We will review unresolved objections before changing their restrictions.

Official tournament records, including participation, results and standings, remain available under the rules that apply to each event. Your email address, login credentials, private match chat, private assistance conversations and restricted replay files stay private.

When you next sign in after the update, we will ask you to acknowledge this information. That acknowledgement leaves your profile visibility unchanged. The new Terms and Privacy Policy have their own acceptance and acknowledgement steps. You can contact us through the Privacy Policy to ask a question, object to publication or ask about account closure.
