# IronClad Caster Programme notice

**Proposed final copy for review. Effective date to be approved with Privacy Policy 1.4.**

IronClad uses the information you provide to review your Caster Programme application, contact you through the platform, manage programme membership and support tournament video publication. Authorised staff can review your submitted information, images and links. Your verified account email and review conversations are private. Discord is optional and helps staff recognise or contact you during review. We use your country during review and show it on a public caster profile only if you choose to display it. A caster invitation or application does not require Steam, verified ELO or a competitive player profile.

Publishing a profile makes the information, media and links shown in the public preview available on IronClad. That can include your display name, languages, games, biography, channel links and verified casting credits. You can change your country visibility in Caster Studio.

When you publish a tournament recording, IronClad shows the title, optional description, recording link and selected tournament, division or match on your caster profile and in Tournament Media. You can unpublish the recording from both places. Authorised staff can hide or correct a recording and retain the action evidence needed for moderation. A recording you publish is separate from coverage that an IronClad administrator has verified.

Closing your account ends Caster Studio access and withdraws your current public profile, its media and promotion, and recordings you published. Public tournament history may retain a verified credit labelled **Former Caster**, without a link to your former profile or recording and without your avatar, handle or contact information. The Privacy Policy explains how we review retention, requests and holds, and which minimal closed-account and historical evidence may remain.

IronClad loads an embedded video player when you choose to load it. YouTube or Twitch then receives the information it needs to deliver the player, which may include your network address, device and browser information. Its privacy terms apply. If you choose the watch link, you open the provider directly.

For privacy requests, corrections or concerns, use the contact details in the Privacy Policy.
