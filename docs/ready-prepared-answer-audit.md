# Ready prepared-answer audit on protected Staging

This inventory follows the 28 question choices in the current cloud menus. Each row was checked against the cited section of the official document or the Stage rules hub. The page numbers are the first relevant page that opens in the PDF viewer. Rulebook and PPA page numbers distinguish legacy v3.0/v3.1 from current v3.2 where pagination changed. The authoritative document governs if the summary and the document differ.

| Menu path and question | Source and check |

| Results & replays → Confirm or dispute a result → How do results and confirmations work? | Rulebook §§ 11.1–11.3 and 11.7–11.11, p. 9. Verified series result, one replay per game and the opponent’s confirmation or dispute window. |
| Results & replays → Confirm or dispute a result → How do I dispute a result? | Rulebook §§ 12.1–12.3 and 11.7–11.10, legacy p. 9 and current p. 10. Verified that a player submits a reason within the available window and an admin reviews the evidence. Corrected the legacy link. |
| Results & replays → Why can’t I submit? | Rulebook §§ 11.1–11.6, p. 9, and the Match workspace. A player’s actual ability to submit depends on the active match, opponent and platform controls. The answer gives guidance and a route to the workspace or help. |
| Results & replays → Replay requirements | Rulebook §§ 11.2–11.6, p. 9. One unique CoH3 .rec file per game played, up to 10 MiB per file. Verified that screenshots cannot replace required replay evidence. |
| Rules & match help → Disconnects & lag | Rulebook §§ 10.4–10.7 and 10.1–10.2, p. 9. Verified the 10:00 boundary, exceptions and the need for an admin ruling when appropriate. |

| Rules & match help → Pauses & deadlines → When should I contact my opponent? | Rulebook §§ 9.1–9.9, p. 8. Verified normal first contact within 24 hours of activation, assistance after 48 hours or sooner if the deadline is at risk, and that these timings alone do not award a result. |
