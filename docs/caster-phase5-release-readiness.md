## Ready budget decision

The active [Ready route](../app/api/ready/route.ts) serves deterministic, source-backed catalogue answers through `resolveReadyAnswer` and stamps `X-Ready-AI-Attempted: 0`. The optional model adapter has no runtime caller in this candidate. Both catalogs lack `ironclad_private.ready_ai_budget` and `public.reserve_ready_ai_usage`, and neither ledger contains a Ready budget receipt. Treat [20261116121500_ready_ai_budget.sql](../supabase/migrations/20261116121500_ready_ai_budget.sql) (SHA-256 `055e0d2fdb7a4f7b7d779527d92d7d5825ff9a845817265876240493a6b1b77c`) as deferred trial work. Deterministic Ready adds no Workers AI inference charges. The account’s plan, other workloads and total infrastructure charges were outside this audit. The optional Ready usage consent, suggestions and Wardrobe have separate schema dependencies and need individual release-scope decisions. [Ready Staging and trial notes](ready-staging.md)

A future optional AI trial needs explicit approval, a scoped provider and model evaluation, the private budget table and service-only reservation RPC tested on an isolated database, and a verified account-level free-plan and same-day usage reading. The current trial is restricted to the Staging identity and correct UTC day. It limits reservations to 2,000 estimated Neurons per account per UTC day, with daily request caps of 100 per provider account, 24 per user and 18 per session, and minute caps of 10, 5 and 4. Concurrent calls reserve before dispatch. A failed or ambiguous provider call retains its reservation. Missing limits, unavailable reservations, or translation being enabled or unspecified stop provider calls. The translation switch is explicitly off in Staging and absent in Production. Cloudflare publishes a 10,000-Neuron account allowance per UTC day on Workers Free, with additional capacity subject to paid-plan pricing. Use provider dashboard totals and measured consumption to assess shared headroom. Treat reservations as an internal conservative estimate.
